Proof of the Courtade–Kumar most informative Boolean function conjecture, mostly AI-generated and checked in Lean

Researchers at Google and the Chinese University of Hong Kong give a computer-assisted proof, verified end to end in Lean, that no Boolean function of nn bits sent through a binary symmetric channel carries more than 1−h(α)1 - h(\alpha) bits of mutual information with the noisy output, the value attained by a dictator, and write that the overwhelming majority of the new ideas and results were generated by AI.

What was found

Courtade and Kumar conjectured in 2013 that for XX uniform on {0,1}n\{0, 1\}^n and YY obtained by flipping each bit independently with probability α\alpha, every Boolean ff satisfies I(f(X);Y)≤1−h(α)I(f(X); Y) \le 1 - h(\alpha), where hh is binary entropy, and verified it numerically up to dimension seven. The proof builds on the differential-equation method of the CUHK group, which reduces the problem to a finite-dimensional optimization given the right Bellman candidate function: differentiation along the noise semigroup writes entropy production as an average of edge costs, and the key estimate becomes an unrestricted Bellman inequality with two mean and two entropy constraints. Part 1 proves the balanced case and Part 2 the unbalanced one, with validated interval arithmetic for the computational lemmas. The Lean project has about 45,500 modules and needed roughly 1,200 CPU-hours to check; its 63-line statement file is also in Google DeepMind's Formal Conjectures repository.

Novelty check

arXiv, 2026-11-23: the paper is 2609.24931 (v1 21 September, v2 24 September 2026). Two other proofs appeared within days: Vu Khac Ky and Tuan Tran, Dictators are most informative (2609.24184, 21 September 2026), human-written with a different entropy-production and spectral method, posted in coordination with this paper; and Mahdavifar and Beirami (2609.26444, 22 September 2026), whose disclosure says generative AI produced the detailed derivations. The paper also mentions an outline with Lean files shared privately by a further group. Within this registry no entry covered the conjecture.

Caveats and known objections

Graded formal: the whole proof, numerical certificates included, is checked in Lean against a short statement that was independently added to Formal Conjectures (pull request 6688, merged 15 October 2026). The repository reports full Comparator runs accepted on 4 and 11 October 2026 and acceptance by the con-leche kernel on 10 October; all of these were run by the authors' team or their agents, not by an outside party. The paper is not refereed. Priority is shared: a human-written proof by a different method was posted the same day by agreement, so the result does not rest on this proof alone. Autonomy is collaborative. The paper's Human-AI collaboration section says the AI produced the overwhelming majority of the novel ideas and results and handled the step from the balanced to the unbalanced case entirely without human input, but the CUHK team supplied the max-phi Bellman function that gave the overall proof strategy and one principal lower bound, and human direction was needed in other phases, so neither side would have finished alone. Gemini's first contribution was counterexamples to a generalization of the conjecture, not to the conjecture itself.
