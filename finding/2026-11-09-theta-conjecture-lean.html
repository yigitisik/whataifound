Proof of the Theta conjecture and the valley Delta conjecture, developed with ChatGPT and checked in Lean

Five mathematicians at Nankai University prove the touching refinement of the Theta conjecture of Interdonato and Iraci, and with it the valley version of the Delta conjecture of Haglund, Remmel and Wilson, saying the derivations and proofs were developed with ChatGPT 6 Astra; a separate Lean proof written with OpenAI Codex checks the main theorem and the Theta conjecture against a statement that imports only Mathlib.

What was found

The Delta conjecture gives a combinatorial formula for a Delta operator applied to ene_n in terms of decorated labelled Dyck paths, in a rise and a valley version; the rise version has been proved, while the valley version remained open. D'Adderio and coauthors introduced the Theta operators, and Interdonato and Iraci conjectured a formula for ΘeℓΘek∇ed\Theta_{e_\ell}\Theta_{e_k}\nabla e_d as a sum over parking functions carrying both decorated rises and decorated contractible valleys, which contains both versions, together with a refinement by the touch number. The paper proves the refinement (its Theorem 1.4) by comparing an algebraic recurrence, from an endomorphism of the Dyck path algebra of Carlsson and Mellit, with a combinatorial one obtained by introducing signed labels and deleting the main diagonal. Summing over the touch number gives the Theta conjecture, and the corollaries give the valley Delta conjecture and the generalized valley Delta conjecture of Qiu and Wilson. The Lean repository states ten results in a challenge file that imports only Mathlib and defines the modified Macdonald polynomials by Haiman's characterization, the operators and the decorated parking functions itself; the 849-module proof follows a different route from the manuscript, through a recursion in a polynomial model of the Dyck path algebra.

Caveats and known objections

Graded formal for exactly the two statements checked, the touching refinement and the Theta conjecture itself. The repository reports that lake comparator passed, that a paranoid run with lean4lean, nanoda, con-leche and con-ron passed, and that negative controls are rejected; all of these were run by the authors, and there is no Palomar record or outside run. The challenge file has to define modified Macdonald polynomials, the Theta operators and decorated parking functions from scratch, so the check is only as good as those transcriptions, which the repository supports with existence and uniqueness theorems that rule out vacuity. The Delta-operator forms of the corollaries, including the valley Delta conjecture as usually stated, are not formalized; they follow on paper by summing and specializing. The manuscript is an unrefereed preprint. Autonomy is ai-assisted on the strictest reading: the paper says the derivations and proofs were developed with the help of ChatGPT 6 Astra and that the authors studied and checked all of it and wrote the paper themselves, which leaves the share of the key ideas unstated, and the Lean library was written by Dun Qiu with Codex. A reading of the AI's role as collaborative is defensible, so the weaker grade wins. The repository README spells the first author's name Haijun.
