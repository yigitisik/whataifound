Sharp logarithmic Kakeya maximal and tube bounds in every dimension, registered as a Lean proof

Mark Lewko, with ChatGPT and Codex doing the mathematical development and Lean implementation, registered on Palomar a Lean proof that the Kakeya maximal operator is bounded from Ln(ℝn)L^n(\mathbb{R}^n) to Ln(Sn−1)L^n(S^{n-1}) with norm at most Cn[log⁡(2/δ)](n−1)/nC_n[\log(2/\delta)]^{(n-1)/n} in every dimension n≥2n \ge 2, a logarithmic power the accompanying notes argue is optimal.

What was found

For 0<δ≤10 < \delta \le 1 the Kakeya maximal function takes the largest average of |f||f| over radius-δ\delta neighbourhoods of unit segments in each direction. The two Lean theorems are the maximal bound ‖Kδf‖Ln(Sn−1)≤Cn[log⁡(2/δ)](n−1)/n‖f‖Ln(ℝn)\|K_\delta f\|_{L^n(S^{n-1})} \le C_n[\log(2/\delta)]^{(n-1)/n}\|f\|_{L^n(\mathbb{R}^n)} and a weighted tube bound: for δ\delta-separated directions, arbitrary centres and nonnegative weights, ∫(∑jaj1Tj)n/(n−1)≤Cnlog⁡(2/δ)δn−1∑jajn/(n−1)\int (\sum_j a_j 1_{T_j})^{n/(n-1)} \le C_n \log(2/\delta)\,\delta^{n-1}\sum_j a_j^{n/(n-1)}. By Hölder's inequality the δ\delta-neighbourhood of every Kakeya set then has volume at least cn/[log⁡(2/δ)]n−1c_n/[\log(2/\delta)]^{n-1}, which matches the construction of Fernández-Delgado and de la Salle, and the estimates imply the Kakeya maximal function conjecture and full Minkowski dimension for every bounded Kakeya set. Those consequences and the matching lower bound are written arguments, not Lean theorems. The development reuses 1,682 files of OpenAI's Lean library at a pinned commit, which the record calls substantial reuse rather than an independent implementation of the analytic infrastructure.

Novelty check

arXiv, 2026-11-19, abs:Kakeya AND abs:logarithmic AND abs:maximal and abs:Kakeya AND abs:"maximal function" AND abs:"every dimension" both returned no records. Within this registry, 2026-10-26-local-smoothing-all-dimensions records OpenAI's claim, with its own unaudited Lean formalization, of the Kakeya maximal conjecture in every dimension as a consequence of local smoothing, and 2026-10-06-kakeya-3d-maximal-4d records the three-dimensional maximal and four-dimensional set claims; this registration is a different, sharper logarithmic statement by a different author, and it does not check either of those manuscripts. The 2026-11-20 watch run surfaced it through its Palomar registration.

Caveats and known objections

Held at claimed on a conservative reading. Palomar records trust level high and checks by the nanoda and con-ron kernels of KakeyaPalomar.sharpMaximalBound and KakeyaPalomar.sharpTubeBound against a 58-line Mathlib-only Challenge, which for other entries in this registry has supported formal. Against that: no human outside the author has audited the Challenge statements; the registry's review and the repository's own review were done by models; the repository README still says the candidate has not been registered and describes a failed preflight; and the geometric consequences that make the result notable are not formalized. There is no paper; the mathematics is in the repository's notes and a working manuscript. The Kakeya maximal conjecture in every dimension is a major claim, and nobody in harmonic analysis is on record as having read this. Autonomy is ai-assisted on the record's own wording, which credits Lewko with research direction and formulation and the models with 'AI-assisted mathematical development and Lean implementation'; collaborative or ai-led are arguable, and the weaker grade wins.
