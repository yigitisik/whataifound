Locked AI model for p16/Ki-67 dual-stain triage validated on 9,006 cervical screening cases

Cytoreader-Fusion, an AI system that classifies p16/Ki-67 dual-stain cytology slides to triage HPV-positive screening results, was locked before validation and then detected biopsy-confirmed CIN 3+ with 88.3% sensitivity and 54.4% specificity in a held-out cohort of 4,996 cases.

Original work

DOINEJM AI: Closing the Automation Gap in HPV-Based Cervical Cancer Screening, independent external validation of an AI model for dual-stain triage↗

What was found

Dual-stain cytology is the guideline-endorsed triage for HPV-positive screening results but depends on manual reading. The ensemble model was trained on 1,919 cases and evaluated under blinded honest-broker oversight on 9,006 cases in four cohorts, with all preprocessing, aggregation rules and decision parameters fixed before validation. The endpoint was histologically confirmed CIN 3+, or AIN 3+ in an anal cohort, rather than agreement with cytologists, and one classifier served both ThinPrep and SurePath slides without retraining. In the post-lock cohort sensitivity was 88.3% (90% interval 85.3 to 91.3) and specificity 54.4% (52.9 to 55.9); across cohorts the authors report both above ASC-US+ Pap cytology and comparable to or exceeding manual dual-stain reading.

Novelty check

Europe PMC, 2026-10-29: TITLE_ABS:"dual stain" AND TITLE_ABS:"artificial intelligence" AND TITLE_ABS:"cervical" returned 3 records: this study, a 2024 review, and a 2021 JNCI paper on deep-learning automation of dual-stain cytology by Wentzensen, Lahrmann, Clarke and colleagues, several of the same investigators. Automated dual-stain reading is therefore not new; what this adds is a model locked in advance and evaluated on a held-out cohort against histological endpoints.

Caveats and known objections

External in data, not in investigators: the title calls the validation independent, and the post-lock cohort was held out under an honest broker, but the authors include the group behind the 2021 automation work and staff of H-Labs GmbH, so it is not independent in this registry's sense. Funded by the US National Cancer Institute and others, with Kaiser Permanente Northern California among the participating institutions. Retrospective, on archived slides; the endpoint is a hard histological one, but no patient was managed on the model's output. Specificity of about 54% in the post-lock cohort implies many referrals, and the abstract does not give the manual dual-stain figures for that cohort. Whether the model is available outside the developers is not stated. Autonomy is ai-assisted: the system classifies slides for a triage decision clinicians make.
