Claimed Diophantine definition of ℤ in ℚ

Elliot Glazer, working with four frontier models, published an explicit integer-coefficient polynomial whose rational zeros project exactly onto the integers, which if correct would refute Mazur's conjecture on the topology of rational points and give a second route to the undecidability of Hilbert's tenth problem over the rationals.

What was found

A set is Diophantine in ℚ\mathbb{Q} when membership can be written as the existence of rational witnesses satisfying a polynomial equation. Julia Robinson defined ℤ\mathbb{Z} in ℚ\mathbb{Q} in 1949 using both quantifiers, and Koenigsmann later gave a universal definition, but a purely existential one stayed open and Mazur's conjecture on the real closure of rational points would have ruled it out. The repository gives a construction in 292,890,231,905292{,}890{,}231{,}905 variables and two smaller quartics, one in 411 variables with every coefficient written out in decimal and one in 394 variables written against the fixed integer C=3225C = 3^{2^{25}}, and argues that degree four is the minimum possible. The work builds on OpenAI's October 6 preprint, which claims the undecidability of Hilbert's tenth problem over ℚ\mathbb{Q} by a different route that does not produce a Diophantine definition of the integers, and credits a rank-one elliptic curve strategy going back to Thanases Pheidas and a prime-pattern strategy to Zhi-Wei Sun.

Novelty check

The claim is new in this registry and distinct from 2026-10-06-openai-math-collection, which carries OpenAI's claimed negative answer to Hilbert's tenth problem over the rationals: that release proves undecidability through a compactness and height argument without exhibiting a Diophantine definition of ℤ\mathbb{Z}, and this manuscript's own introduction treats it as an input rather than a duplicate. arXiv, 2026-11-09: abs:"Diophantine" AND abs:"rational" AND abs:"integers" AND abs:"Hilbert" returned 4 records and abs:"Mazur" AND abs:"Diophantine" AND abs:"rational" returned 3, none of them a Diophantine definition of the integers in the rationals; the closest are Shlapentokh's 2004 work on large subrings and Poonen's subring results, both weaker than the field. Recent unconditional work by Koymans and Pagano and by Alpöge, Bhargava, Ho and Shnidman settles the analogous question for rings of integers of number fields, which are not ℚ\mathbb{Q}. The manuscript is not on arXiv and has no DOI; the repository is the only artifact.

Caveats and known objections

Capped at claimed, and the strongest caveat is the author's own: the README states that AI review and successful finite checks do not constitute independent human peer review or a completed formal proof. The Lean formalization is incomplete, with 48 accepted helper modules but a main bounded-polynomial declaration that still contains sorry. The finite checks verify the source program, the coordinate changes, the eliminations and the coefficient tables, and the README says plainly that they do not prove the source system's arithmetic characterization of the integers, which is the mathematical content; that argument rests in the manuscript and has no outside confirmation. The repository was created on 2026-11-04 and had reached Version 13 by 2026-11-07, with the variable count falling from roughly 293 billion to 411 across thirteen versions and nine earlier review corrections recorded, so the construction is still moving. The manuscript's author field and the BibTeX author field are deliberately blank. No independent mathematician is on record confirming the proof; the one public exchange on r/math concerns the writing quality and the norms for AI-generated papers rather than the mathematics, and Glazer's reply that the proof is correct is an unverified self-report. The inventory also records that applications of Dodos-Kanellopoulos and Kollar were checked against author versions rather than final published text. Autonomy is collaborative: a named human directed the project and human collaborators contributed the Version 9 reductions and the collective agentic notes the construction relies on, while the models drafted and audited.
