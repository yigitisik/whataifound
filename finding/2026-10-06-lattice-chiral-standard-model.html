Gauge-invariant lattice measure for chiral fermions including the Standard Model, with an Astra-led proof

Nathaniel Craig constructs a smooth, gauge-invariant measure for overlap fermions on four-dimensional Euclidean lattices for a class of nonabelian chiral gauge theories that includes the Standard Model, giving a candidate nonperturbative lattice definition of it, and writes that OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra was essential and that the current proof is Astra-led.

What was found

Left- and right-handed fermions carry different gauge charges in the weak interactions, and no lattice regulator respecting both gauge invariance and chiral symmetry has been known for nonabelian chiral theories in four dimensions, so the Standard Model has lacked a nonperturbative definition. Overlap fermions with Lüscher's reconstruction theorem settled the abelian case. Here the measure current is built by comparing chiral projectors at successive lattice spacings: a smooth connection is reconstructed locally from the links on admissible gauge fields and sampled on finer lattices, and the paper proves the current is exponentially local and satisfies the integrability conditions Lüscher's reconstruction needs, then restricts to the four possible global forms of the Standard Model gauge group. Verification is described on three fronts: a Lean formalization of the mathematical companion (sixteen modules, standard axioms, kernel replay), an analogous two-dimensional construction compared numerically with determinant phases, and a second construction in the opposite order that agrees up to local counterterms.

Novelty check

arXiv, 2026-11-23: the paper is 2610.09093, posted on 6 October 2026 and publicized in an OpenAI post on 15 November; the Lean companion is on Zenodo from the same day. Its introduction places it after Ginsparg-Wilson, Narayanan-Neuberger and Lüscher's abelian construction, and presents the nonabelian four-dimensional case as open. Within this registry 2026-10-26-yang-mills-existence-claim is the nearest entry, a different result (existence of pure Yang-Mills theory as a continuum limit, with no fermions) from a different OpenAI effort.

Caveats and known objections

Capped at claimed. The Lean formalization was carried out by Astra and its correspondence with the mathematical companion was reviewed by AI agents, as the paper says, so no human or outside party has checked that the formal statements say what the physics needs; the definitions are the author's own, not Mathlib's. Three lattice theorists are thanked, Golterman and Shamir throughout the project and Kronfeld for comments on the manuscript, but none is an author and this is not peer review. On scope the author is explicit: the construction is one of principle rather than practice, a phase freedom on disconnected components remains, reflection positivity compatible with admissibility is not established, and the interacting continuum theory remains open. Autonomy is collaborative: the acknowledgements say Craig proposed the strategy of extending Lüscher's abelian result by refinement methods, which went through several rounds with Astra and outside experts before arriving at the current Astra-led proof, and that the manuscript combines author-written sections with extensive rewrites of Astra's proof. A human strategy and an AI-led proof is genuine back-and-forth.
