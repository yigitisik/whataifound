Proof of Barnette's conjecture on Hamiltonian cycles in cubic bipartite planar graphs

An OpenAI manuscript claims that every cubic, bipartite, planar, 3-connected graph has a Hamiltonian cycle, proving a conjecture recorded in 1969, with a Lean formalization of the full statement attached.

What was found

Barnette's conjecture, recorded by Grünbaum in the 1969 Waterloo proceedings, is the bipartite survivor of Tait's conjecture, which Tutte disproved in 1946. Earlier work settled it under face-size restrictions: Goodey for faces of size four and six, Feder and Subi for a broader face colouring, and Schnieders in 2025 for faces of size at most eight. The manuscript passes to the dual triangulation, where by the Alt, Payne, Schmidt and Wood formulation Hamiltonicity becomes a partition of the vertices into two induced trees, and analyses one finite exponential sum over pairs of Tutte states in two ways: pairs whose associated edge set contains a cycle cancel in sign, which forces a pair giving a forest. The proof also yields the known equivalent strengthenings that a Hamiltonian cycle can avoid any prescribed edge and that every three-edge path in a cubic 3-connected bipartite Pfaffian graph lies on one. The family's Lean scope document says the formalization proves the conjecture for every such graph.

Novelty check

Read the introduction and Lean scope at commit fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb on 2026-10-12. Its account of partial results through Schnieders (2025) matches the literature it cites, and no earlier claimed proof was found in the registry. A Hacker News commenter who said they had worked on the conjecture for 24 years reacted to the release in a comment that was quoted widely, but that is reaction rather than a check.

Caveats and known objections

Lean formalization is attached through a Comparator statement, BarnetteHamiltonian.lean, but the catalogue records its review status as unchecked and no outside audit of how the statement encodes planarity and 3-connectivity was found; that encoding is where a formal statement of this conjecture could quietly diverge from the real one. Nobody outside OpenAI is on record as having read the proof. Autonomy is graded ai-led for the same reasons as the other families split from this release.
