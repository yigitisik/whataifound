Smale's mean value constant shown to equal 4, with a Lean proof

Dave Goel, steering Claude and ChatGPT in alternation, proved that the supremum over all degrees of Smale's mean value constant is exactly 4, Smale's own 1981 bound, approached as the degree grows but never attained, so no smaller constant works for every degree; the main theorem is registered on Palomar as a Lean proof.

What was found

For a complex polynomial pp of degree d≥2d \ge 2 with p(0)=0p(0) = 0 and p′(0)≠0p'(0) \ne 0, let S(p)S(p) be the least value of |p(c)/(cp′(0))||p(c)/(c\,p'(0))| over the critical points cc, and let K(d)K(d) be the supremum of S(p)S(p) in degree dd. Smale proved K(d)≤4K(d) \le 4 in 1981 and asked whether 4 could be replaced by 1. That conjecture was refuted earlier in 2026, which this registry records at 2026-09-03-smale-mean-value-k1, and lower bounds then rose to 1.1018 (Jatar and Ng) and 1.1559 (Goel), with Jatar and Ng listing whether the supremum K∗K^* equals 4 as open. The paper proves K∗=limd→∞⁡K(d)=4K^* = \lim_{d \to \infty} K(d) = 4 by combining Jatar and Ng's reduction to admissible univalent Blaschke pairs with an explicit construction. Since each K(d)K(d) is known to be below 4, the supremum is not attained. The Lean development proves the main theorem in the paper's normalization together with Smale's bound K(d)≤4K(d) \le 4, that K(d)K(d) does not decrease with dd, and that K(d)<4K(d) < 4 in every degree.

Novelty check

arXiv, 2026-11-16, abs:"mean value" AND abs:Smale AND abs:constant returned 3 records: this paper (2611.17373), Jatar and Ng's 2610.21472, which states the exact value as open, and a 2004 paper on mean value conjectures for rational maps. Within this registry 2026-09-03-smale-mean-value-k1 is a different and weaker result: it shows the constant exceeds 1, while this one determines its value. vibemathed records the result as solved on 6 November 2026, earlier than the arXiv posting used as the date here, probably from a Zenodo version; that date was not confirmed for this entry.

Caveats and known objections

Graded formal on the Palomar registration, which records trust level high for three theorems at a pinned commit. vibemathed's check read the Lean definitions of K(d) and its supremum line by line and found that they match the problem as Smale and Jatar and Ng pose it; Google DeepMind's Formal Conjectures states only the K = 1 form, so nothing written independently anchors this statement. The paper is unrefereed, and Hexagon labels it a mix of human-written and AI-generated text with human understanding of some parts. The proof gives no bound on the degree needed for K(d)K(d) to exceed a given value below 4. Autonomy is collaborative: the author writes that the work was done overwhelmingly by AI models but steered by him in a continuous critical dialogue, which could support ai-led, and the weaker grade wins when the reading is arguable. Model versions are not stated.
