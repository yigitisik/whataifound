No-go theorem for high-dimensional photonic GHZ states from pair sources, proof found by AlphaProof Nexus

A Lean proof found by Google DeepMind's AlphaProof Nexus agent shows that no complex-weighted monochromatic quantum graph exists on an even number N≥4N \ge 4 of vertices with NN colours, so photonic Greenberger-Horne-Zeilinger states of NN photons in NN or more dimensions cannot be made this way from probabilistic pair sources; six researchers have now explained the proof in a preprint.

What was found

In the graph picture of photonic experiments, vertices are photon paths, coloured and weighted edges are photon pairs, and conditioning on one photon per detector turns each output amplitude into a coherent sum over perfect matchings. A GHZ state of NN photons in DD dimensions then corresponds to a monochromatic quantum graph, one whose matchings cancel on every non-monochromatic colouring and give equal weight to the DD monochromatic ones. The 2017 conjecture of Krenn and Gu bounds the achievable DD; earlier results covered nonnegative weights (Bogdanov), graphs without bichromatic edges or multi-edges, and sparse or cubic graphs. The proof builds a weighted sum of amplitudes whose coefficients factor vertex by vertex, so summing over the colours at one vertex reduces the system to an (N−1)×N(N-1) \times N matrix of effective edge weights, whose row and column imbalance makes the sum vanish on any graph but not on the GHZ target. Since a graph for more than NN colours restricts to one for NN, the preprint states the result for every D≥ND \ge N.

Novelty check

Formal Conjectures, 2026-11-26: FormalConjectures/Paper/MonochromaticQuantumGraph.lean marks eqSystem_no_solution_even_ge4_d_eq_n_explicit solved by 'the DeepMind prover agent' at commit af88acbf of 28 April 2026, and google-deepmind/alphaproof-nexus-results carries the file under APNOutputs/AICollaborator/QuantumOptics from its 19 May release. The preprint's table of results lists nothing unconditional for even NN before April 2026, and two later AI-assisted formal proofs of overlapping statements, by Kenta Kitamura in July 2026 with ChatGPT and Codex and by Rishi Rajasekaran in September 2026 with Codex and Claude, both described as not human-digested. This registry's 2026-05-21-alphaproof-nexus entry covers the system's Erdős and OEIS results and does not mention this one; no other entry covers the conjecture.

Caveats and known objections

Graded formal for the Lean statement, which belongs to the Formal Conjectures project and was written before the proof: it defines the perfect-matching sum recursively over ℂ\mathbb{C}-valued edge weights, and the preprint walks through that encoding, which is what a reader has to trust. The registry has not rebuilt the proof. The theorem covers D≥ND \ge N only; the intermediate range below NN for even N≥6N \ge 6 remains open, and the conjecture over the reals and integers is stated separately in the same file. The preprint, posted on 19 November 2026 by authors including the conjecture's originator and DeepMind staff, is the first human account of the argument and is not refereed. Autonomy is search-scaffold, as for the other AlphaProof Nexus results in this registry: the agent found the proof without human guidance, but humans built the system and supplied the formal target.
