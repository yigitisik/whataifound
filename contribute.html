Grade challenge

Grades move on evidence, never on opinion. That is why the citation below is required: without one there is nothing for a maintainer to weigh, and the entry is never deleted, only downgraded and annotated.

Currently

The paper, rebuttal, retraction or thread that makes the case. This is added to the entry as a challenge source.

Why the citation supports the grade you are proposing. This becomes the note on the revision record, so write it to be read later.

Being an author, or working at the lab, does not disqualify a challenge. An undeclared conflict found later does.

Correction

Small factual fixes that leave both grades alone. Anything that would move a grade is a challenge, and anything larger is better sent as a pull request where the whole diff is visible.

This is recorded on the entry as a correction, because a registry that quietly edits itself is worth less than one that does not.

Editorial policy

Announcements enter as claimed, however confident they sound. Nothing is ever deleted: entries are downgraded and annotated, and the history stays public. Every entry carries a novelty check, including when it comes back clean.
