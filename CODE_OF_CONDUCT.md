# Code of conduct

This project grades other people's scientific work in public. Doing that fairly requires being
able to say "this claim does not hold up" without it becoming an attack on the person who made it,
and to hear the same about our own grades. Everything below follows from that.

## Expected

- **Argue about evidence, not people.** "This entry cites a paper that does not contain the
  result" is the kind of sentence this project runs on. "The submitter is careless" is not.
- **Bring a citation.** Disagreement is welcome and is the point of the grade-challenge and
  independent-check forms. Disagreement without evidence is noise, and will be closed as such.
- **Declare conflicts.** Contributing an entry about your own work, or your employer's, is welcome
  and common. Saying so is expected. An undeclared conflict discovered later damages the registry's
  credibility more than the entry was ever worth.
- **Accept a downgrade gracefully.** Entries are never deleted; they are downgraded on the record
  with the objection kept beside them. That applies to entries about your own work too.
- **Assume competence.** Contributors here span mathematics, biology, machine learning and
  software. Someone unfamiliar with your subfield's conventions is not thereby unqualified.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, discussions, or commit
  messages.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments based on who someone is: race, ethnicity, nationality,
  gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, religion, or academic pedigree.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled arguments without new evidence, or brigading a grade.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Submitting entries in bad faith: fabricated citations, sources that do not say what the entry
  claims, or coordinated grade inflation for a lab or a person.

## Scope

Applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any
private correspondence about the project.

## Enforcement

Report to misik6@gatech.edu, or privately to any maintainer listed in
[CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff). Reports are handled confidentially. If your report concerns a
maintainer, send it to a different one.

Maintainers will respond with what the situation warrants: a private word, an edit or removal of
the content, a temporary block, or a permanent ban. Whoever acts will say what they did and why, to
the other maintainers at minimum. A maintainer does not decide a report about their own conduct;
see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

Bad-faith entries are a content problem as well as a conduct one. The entry gets corrected or
downgraded on the record either way, because the record is what the project is.
